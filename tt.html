Understanding Emissions

Emissions from industrial activities like coal mining are a primary driver of climate change. The main greenhouse gases involved are Carbon Dioxide (CO₂) and Methane (CH₄).

Impact of Emissions

  • Climate Change: Greenhouse gases trap heat in the atmosphere, leading to global warming, extreme weather events, and rising sea levels.
  • Air Quality: Pollutants can cause respiratory problems and other health issues in nearby communities.
  • Environmental Degradation: Mining activities can affect local ecosystems, water quality, and land stability.

Key Reduction Strategies

  • Methane Capture: Capturing methane before it's released and using it for energy generation prevents a potent greenhouse gas from entering the atmosphere.
  • Electrification: Replacing diesel-powered vehicles and machinery with electric alternatives reduces direct emissions and can be powered by renewable sources.
  • Energy Efficiency: Optimizing processes to use less energy reduces indirect emissions from electricity consumption.
  • Renewable Energy: Transitioning to solar, wind, or other renewable energy sources drastically cuts down the carbon footprint of a mine.
